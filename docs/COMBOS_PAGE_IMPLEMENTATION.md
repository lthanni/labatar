# Combos page implementation

The existing desktop **Combos** tab is the first target. The page uses the published Worker catalog and its IndexedDB cache, while a separate browser-local workspace stores personal tracking and local combos. The design reference remains [COMBOS_PAGE_CONSUMPTION_DESIGN.md](COMBOS_PAGE_CONSUMPTION_DESIGN.md).

The local MP4 workflow and recording-library reuse are described in [COMBOS_PAGE_LOCAL_VIDEO_PLAN.md](COMBOS_PAGE_LOCAL_VIDEO_PLAN.md).

Browser previews of the implementation: [My gameplan](combos-page-mockup/preview-implementation-gameplan.png), [Raw](combos-page-mockup/preview-implementation-raw.png), [editor review](combos-page-mockup/preview-implementation-submit.png), and [narrow layout](combos-page-mockup/preview-implementation-narrow.png).

## Implemented in source

- **My gameplan** has selected character and support portraits, portrait pickers, one grouped starter selection with a distinct Crits group for starters such as `2B!`, Autopilot and Situational sections that remain visible when empty, and a New combos section for untracked routes and local drafts under the selected starter. It also has Candidates, Maybe someday, Retired, and Dismissed sections, plus personal status, role, condition, name, difficulty, tags, notes, and dismissal reason. A separate Candidate chip beside the status filters shows only upstream candidates while preserving the selected Learning, Considering, or other personal status filter; their cards carry a Candidate tag.
- Nonzero blue local-combo counts and pink upstream-candidate counts appear below the selected character and support portraits, each option in their pickers, and each starter button. A character count includes all its supports; a support count uses the selected character; a starter count uses the selected character and support. Local counts include saved local revision drafts, and candidate counts appear once upstream candidates are loaded. Uploaded and rejected candidates are excluded from the pink counts. If an active candidate for the selected starter is hidden by status or tag filters, the gameplan shows a shortcut to reveal it.
- **Raw** starts with all available routes, including local routes, and offers source, pairing, starter, position, status, hitconfirm, counterhit, editor-pick, resource, damage, and difficulty filters, removable applied-filter chips, sortable columns, and a column chooser. Unknown published facts stay distinct from explicit “no.”
- Local combos can be created, edited, deleted, and tracked in the same workspace. A My local combos button opens all of them in Raw; selecting a local combo exposes edit and submission controls in its detail panel. Their IDs are separate from catalog IDs. Saved catalog routes retain a small display snapshot if a catalog record disappears.
- The shared detail panel starts with read-only displayed values, including the effective name and difficulty, route, media, tracking labels, personal notes, and editor notes. Editable controls follow in order: private name, difficulty, status, role, condition, tags, and notes; local MP4 controls; then combo and catalog editing, candidate review, and publication. Existing local combo edits no longer repeat private tracking fields in the combo-data dialog. Clearing a private name or difficulty restores the editor value where one exists.
- The shared combo detail panel can record a new MP4 through the existing OBS manual recording flow, then automatically link the finished recording to the selected combo. It uses the character, support, starter, position, and damage in the file name; a pending session can be recovered after a restart or stopped with F9 or OBS. The panel can also link, preview, replace, and unlink a private local MP4 from the desktop recording library. A native file picker can import an outside MP4 into the configured recording folder before linking it. Missing recordings keep their saved association for relinking. Video links remain out of public publisher payloads; publication still uses the YouTube URL.
- Automatic media labels distinguish **No video** (neither a usable local MP4 nor YouTube) from **No YouTube video** (an MP4 exists without YouTube). They appear on gameplan cards, in combo details, and as a Raw column and filter. A YouTube URL on the linked recording also counts as a YouTube video.
- The detail player, media labels, and publisher form now resolve YouTube from the same order: the local combo URL, candidate URL, linked recording URL, then catalog video. The detail labels the source and keeps local MP4 playback separate. The catalog action opens a preparation form even without YouTube so an editor can upload a linked MP4 or paste a URL; its label says this, while final publication still requires a reviewed payload with a valid YouTube URL. A successful YouTube upload from that form also saves the URL to the local combo immediately, including when the editor closes the form before publishing.
- A combo with a linked local MP4 can be submitted as a shared candidate from its detail panel. The submission uploads a snapshot of catalog-facing combo fields and the MP4; personal tracking fields remain local. A local receipt records the candidate ID. Editors load upstream candidates from the Combos toolbar; after the first load, the page reloads them on later visits. When an upstream snapshot still matches the submitted local combo, the Candidate tag and review actions attach to that same combo, preserving its Learning, Considering, or other personal status and avoiding a duplicate card. Changed or unmatched snapshots appear as separate combos that can be tracked independently. Candidates appear in Raw and under their starters in My gameplan. Selecting one shows its video and review actions in the normal combo detail panel. Approval, rejection, MP4 download, YouTube upload, link recovery, and catalog publication stay on that panel. Saving a candidate MP4 to the recording library keeps a local candidate-to-recording link; after a reload the review panel reuses that recording for playback and upload. Older downloads are found by their deterministic candidate filename. The YouTube title defaults to the same character, support, starter, screen position, and damage format used by Record for this combo. Submission is currently public; loading and reviewing upstream candidates requires editor access.
- Catalog publishing and candidate review use the persistent catalog sign-in session. If a catalog preflight, candidate list or sign-in check, or candidate MP4 read times out, the desktop app closes stalled session connections and retries that read once. Editor writes are not retried because a timed-out write may already have succeeded. Publish writes have a timeout and report that their outcome is unknown; refresh the catalog before trying again. Startup diagnostics record publish failures and connection resets.
- Published catalog combos retain the UUID of their source candidate. On catalog refresh, the original submitter's unchanged local draft or an editor-held local candidate copy is replaced by that combo, with personal tracking and its recording link transferred. An original draft edited after submission stays local and offers a review action to replace it explicitly. Older candidate receipts without a saved submission snapshot use exact route, pairing, damage, and position matching. Publishing removes the source candidate and any equivalent duplicate candidate and queued MP4 from the Worker. Candidate listing reconciles older duplicate rows left behind after publication, and the page hides matching candidates if a stale list response arrives.
- Raw can compare a candidate with a current Using route, mark the candidate Using and the prior route Retired, and undo that replacement. The candidate takes the prior role and condition.
- A local combo can be reviewed and submitted through the existing desktop `catalogPublisher` flow. That flow handles Access sign-in, checks the server-authorized editor identity, confirms the payload, and uses revision headers. Move references remain in the saved and published data but are hidden from the local revision form, submission form, and review display. Successful publication removes the local draft and selects the catalog combo; new local combos transfer personal tracking and their recording link, while catalog revisions keep the catalog combo's existing tracking and recording link.
- An approved candidate can upload its queued MP4 to YouTube directly from the review panel; the desktop app saves a local recording copy automatically. After YouTube upload, the desktop app persists the combo and YouTube link as a normal local combo, transfers personal tracking and the recording link, clears its candidate receipt, then deletes the uploaded candidate from the Worker queue. Uploaded rows left by an interrupted cleanup are hidden from candidate cards and counts, then finished on the next candidate load. The local combo remains available for catalog publication. A local combo with a linked MP4 can upload that file to YouTube from the publication form; the resulting URL fills the form and is saved on the recording. Saved editor notes are listed separately from the optional additional-note box. Pick text, move links, and editor notes may legitimately be empty; YouTube remains required for publication.
- A catalog combo can also be opened as a **local revision draft**. The draft keeps the catalog ID, source revision, move links, editor notes, other mechanics, video start, and resource fields while the editor changes the route, editor notes, and public metadata locally. Segment notes that no longer match an edited route must be repaired or detached before publication. Publishing checks the source revision before writing, so a stale draft cannot overwrite a newer catalog revision. After publication, removing a catalog-origin draft preserves the player's tracking on the catalog combo.
- The Worker source, catalog parser, and browser publisher now accept an optional editor name, difficulty (1–5), and editor-pick context and reason. Unnamed records keep the name field empty; the page uses a neutral placeholder where a heading is needed and shows the route separately. Existing records remain unrated when they have no editor difficulty.

## Worker deployment

The updated Worker API and editor assets were deployed to `https://labatar-catalog.landenhanni.workers.dev` on 2026-10-17 (Cloudflare version `b3dec5c1-2b0d-4dd4-be32-9b020a3231e5`). After deployment, `/health` returned `{"ok":true,"features":{"comboNotes":true}}`; public `/v1/combos` and `/v1/moves` returned 12 and 0 records respectively; an unauthenticated request to `/v1/editor/app` redirected to Cloudflare Access. No catalog record was written during this deployment.

The candidate API was deployed on 2026-10-19 (Cloudflare version `cf357e2e-6431-459f-87e8-378ac95bf3d6`). The private `labatar-combo-candidates` R2 bucket and remote D1 `combo_candidates` table and index are in place. Hosted `/health` returned `{"ok":true,"features":{"comboNotes":true,"comboCandidates":true}}`; `/v1/combos` still returned 13 records, and the editor candidate route redirected to Cloudflare Access. A temporary 3-byte multipart upload successfully created and uploaded a part, then aborted; D1 contained no smoke-test row and R2 contained no completed object afterward.
The candidate video range endpoint was deployed on 2026-10-19 (Cloudflare version `13476a53-39e4-444a-a918-eb09235cce8d`). It supports partial MP4 responses so the desktop review player can seek without downloading the entire file. Hosted `/health` still reports candidate storage enabled, and `/v1/combos` still returns 13 records. An authenticated pending candidate MP4 has not yet been played end to end in the desktop app.
The candidate approval endpoint was deployed on 2026-10-19. A direct Wrangler D1 query returned Cloudflare error 7403 despite the signed-in token listing D1 write scope. The initial endpoint attempted an additive `approved_at` migration during review; an approval then appeared to stall. Version `e8192459-039f-4618-add8-06ea18fceccb` removed that runtime schema change and persists approval in an existing field. The desktop client now times out stalled editor requests and records list and approval failures in `startup-debug.log`. Hosted `/health` still reports candidate storage enabled, and an authenticated live approval has not yet been exercised after the fix.

The uploaded-candidate cleanup endpoint was deployed on 2026-10-19 (Cloudflare version `3ebebe0f-8905-4d08-9356-194977f725f7`). An authenticated `DELETE /v1/editor/candidates/:id` removes only an uploaded candidate; a missing row is treated as already removed so an interrupted cleanup can retry. Hosted `/health` returned candidate storage enabled and `/v1/combos` returned 14 records. The endpoint was covered by a local Worker test; no live candidate was deleted for verification.

Candidate provenance was deployed on 2026-10-19 (Cloudflare version `fdd91c5c-db03-43c9-80cf-80eb8f0929cc`). Published combos can now carry `sourceCandidateId`, which lets the original submitter's local draft move to the catalog identity after refresh. A later catalog revision preserves this ID. Hosted `/health` returned candidate storage enabled. No live catalog record was written to test cross-device replacement; previously published records without provenance cannot be matched automatically.

Candidate resubmissions now carry `originCandidateId`, the UUID of the first submission for that local combo. The Worker returns it on candidate rows and stores it with each candidate snapshot. When an editor saves an uploaded candidate as a local combo, edits its route or other public fields, and publishes it, the catalog record's `sourceCandidateId` remains the first submission ID. Queue cleanup and the original submitter's local-draft replacement use that ID, so they do not depend on the edited combo having identical gameplay fields. Older rows without origin metadata retain the earlier gameplay comparison as a compatibility fallback. New candidates with different origins remain separate even if their gameplay fields match. A later catalog revision retains the same origin ID.

The Worker update was deployed on 2026-10-19 as version `0f4d06ac-f239-4b14-854e-700392ea8b19`; `/health` returned candidate storage enabled. The desktop app must be restarted to send `originCandidateId` on resubmission because its Electron main process handles candidate uploads.

Automatic candidate cleanup was deployed on 2026-10-19 (Cloudflare version `9d944219-f7d1-4f3e-93b9-76ba0e6c2020`). A successful catalog publish now deletes its source candidate row and queued MP4; editor candidate listing reconciles older rows left behind after publication. Hosted `/health` returned candidate storage enabled, and public `/v1/combos` returned 16 records, one with candidate provenance. An authenticated live candidate list and the user's local workspace were not inspected.

Duplicate-route candidate cleanup was deployed on 2026-10-19 (Cloudflare version `e568e95d-5cdf-406a-8ebc-2e3add86630f`). The published 392-damage Aang/Gyatso combo had source candidate `b133cf47-127f-485b-b29c-ee4b2384b096`, but a second pending candidate `8f35768e-4445-4650-8400-58837ae92f15` had the same route, position, damage, costs, mechanics, and starter stance. UUID-only cleanup left the second row visible. The Worker and page now match equivalent gameplay routes while allowing public name and move links to differ. The verified duplicate MP4 and row were removed from remote R2 and D1; a read-only D1 query then showed no 392 candidate rows and one unrelated rejected 381 row. Hosted `/health` remained healthy.
